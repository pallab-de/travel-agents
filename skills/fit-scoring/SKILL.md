# Fit scoring

A guest rating says how happy past guests were. It doesn't say whether a place is right for these people on this trip.
A stylish 4.8 villa with nothing to do is the wrong pick for a group that wants to unwind, when a
4.5 farm with a lake, a games room and a play area is the right one. That judgement is what makes a
recommendation trustworthy, so it's made deliberately, by an agent whose only job it is, and challenged by another.

## The score

**Rank score = guest score + overlays**, clamped to 1-5 (`rank-score-v2`, enforced by fact-guard).
- **Guest score:** the weighted mean of reviews actually read. Verified-stay booking sites count 1.5, review
  platforms 1.0.
- **Overlays:** one per criterion of the agreed fit profile that a place meets (+) or falls foul of (-), or an
  evidenced `universal:<quality>` the profile didn't foresee. Each is at most its criterion's weight (never more
  than 0.5) and together at most ±1.0.

## Procedure

1. **Inputs exist:**
   - `trip_intake.json` with a confirmed fit profile (`trip_intake.py validate` passes);
   - the spec with each card's facts and dossier captured;
   - guest scores computed.
2. **Run the fit scorer**: `stay_spec.py scorer-view PROJECT` writes `scorer_view.json` (the cards in name order, without
   ranks, scores or overlays). Give a fresh sub-agent (Agent tool, `model: "opus"`) `brief-fit-scorer.md`,
   `trip_intake.json`, `scorer_view.json` and the project's evidence (captures, review files, `RESEARCH.md`), not `spec.json`.
   - It never sees the current order or the builder's reasoning.
   - It writes `fit_overlays.json`: `{<card id>: [overlay, ...]}`.
   - It also writes `fit_notes.md`: per place, what it looked for and didn't find. Absence is recorded, not
     guessed.
3. **Capture what an overlay needs.** If the scorer found a quality in the evidence that isn't yet a card fact
   (a lake view mentioned in reviews and visible in the listing photos), add it to `stay_report.json` under `extra_facts` (`{row id: [{label, value, source}]}`, so a rebuild keeps it),
   rebuild (`stay_spec.py build . --store captures`, or `stay_spec.py build . --merge-into spec.json --store captures` in a mixed trip; the new fact is only a row once the spec has it), capture it (`pipeline.py status` names it; `pipeline.py capture`),
   then run `stay_spec.py scorer-view` again and give the same fresh scorer (or a new one) the updated view for those cards to write their overlays.
   Anything measurable, like walking time to the sights, is a Google Maps measurement (`tools/place_distance.py`). An overlay can't rest on a fact the gate hasn't seen.
4. **Apply:** `python "<plugin root>/skills/fit-scoring/scripts/apply_overlays.py" spec.json fit_overlays.json`. `spec.json` is generated by
   `stay_spec.py build`, which discards overlays: run `apply_overlays.py` again after every rebuild.
   - It writes each card's `group_fit`, recomputes its score from `guest_score` and re-orders the cards by rank
     score (the previous order breaks ties).
   - It renumbers the ranks in the cards and the tables.
   - It refuses, naming each overlay and why, anything fact-guard's overlay rules would refuse (criterion, size, sign, one fact
     per overlay, the fact's shape). The gate still checks the captures and the concerns behind it. Fix the overlay or the
     evidence, never the rule.
5. **Then the review panel** (`claim-review`). The judgement reviewer tests every overlay:
   - does the fact really deliver what the criterion wants?
   - is the size fair next to the other places?
   - would this group agree?
   - is a drawback missing that the evidence shows?

## What the code checks, and what it can't

fact-guard refuses an overlay that:
- answers no agreed criterion (and isn't a `universal:` quality);
- has no reason;
- is bigger than its criterion's weight, or has the wrong sign;
- points at no fact, at a section title, or at a negation ("No pool" earning a pool bonus);
- answers a `maps` criterion with something that isn't a map measurement;
- for a drawback, points at a concern that isn't on the card (concerns themselves must be corroborated).

It can't tell whether "lakeside" in a listing title means a lake view from the rooms. That is the judgement
reviewer's job, which is why the panel runs after this skill and before anything is called finished.

## Done when

- `apply_overlays.py` reports every overlay accepted (it applies fact-guard's overlay rules); the project gate passes later, after the captures.
- Every confirmed criterion was looked for at every place (`fit_notes.md` says found, not found, or unclear for
  each).
- The judgement reviewer's ledger tests every overlay (`review_gate.py` requires a `score_reason` claim on every
  card that carries one).
